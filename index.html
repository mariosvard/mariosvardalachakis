Welcome to my digital space.

I design and deploy privacy-preserving AI systems built for regulated environments.

As a Software Engineer (AI/ML), I develop machine learning and deep learning models for privacy and security, integrating differential privacy mechanisms, secure ML pipelines, and Responsible AI compliance within GDPR-aligned frameworks. I architect scalable AI infrastructures, web platforms, and interactive analytical dashboards that enable secure model monitoring, transparency, and data-driven decision-making. My work bridges advanced research with production-grade engineering, ensuring systems are secure, auditable, and interpretable by design.

Through my contributions to Horizon Europe research initiatives, I have helped develop end-to-end AI platforms that translate state-of-the-art methodologies into compliant, production-ready systems operating at scale.
